Questions before you order

Tear-strip mailer boxes: buying questions

Is a presentation sample enough to approve shipping protection?

No. Review a filled pack with the intended insert, closure and shipping label. Agree representative handling or transport tests for your route.

Can inside and outside artwork use the same file?

Both surfaces need a dieline-based layout with correct orientation and safe areas. Keep important graphics clear of creases, closures and tear paths.
