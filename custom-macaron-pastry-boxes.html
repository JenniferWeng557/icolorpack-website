Your product, your specification

Planning your macaron and pastry boxes

Use the product you actually intend to pack as the starting point. These checks help turn a visual reference into an approvable packaging specification.

📐

Size the tray around real pastries

Provide the finished macaron diameter and height or the pastry tray dimensions. Product tolerances matter: a cavity that fits one batch tightly may damage another.

✓

Check dividers and food-contact layers

Review divider height, finger clearance and whether wrappers or liners are used. Confirm window, paper and contact-layer suitability for the intended destination and food.

Customization & approval

Review fit, food-contact layers and handling together

📏

Finished product clearance

Measure the decorated product and its cake board, tray, wrapper or divider. Include protruding decorations and the space needed for safe loading and removal.

🍰

Materials for the intended use

Identify direct food contact, expected grease or moisture and the delivery market. Paper, film, inks and liners must be reviewed as a complete specification; decorative finishes are not a substitute for contact suitability.

📦

Sample and handling approval

Check the packed sample for base support, locking, window attachment and movement. Confirm how it is carried and transported, including any separate support or temperature-control requirements.

Questions before you order

Macaron and pastry boxes: buying questions

Should I order by cake diameter alone?

No. Include the cake board, decorations, total height and loading clearance. For pastry assortments, provide the tray layout and finished piece dimensions.

Can you guarantee food-contact compliance from a photo?

No. A photo does not establish material suitability. Confirm the intended contact, destination and required evidence before selecting the complete packaging specification.

Do you provide design help and a quote?

Yes. We provide free design support and quotations within 24 hours. Send dimensions, quantity, artwork and delivery destination, plus any food-contact or handling requirements.
