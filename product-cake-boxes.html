B2B Sourcing FAQ

Are your materials food-grade certified?

Yes, all our bakery boxes use FDA-compliant ivory board and PET windows. We provide SGS certification reports upon request.

Do you offer custom sizing?

Absolutely. Beyond our standard 4", 6", 8", and 10" sizes, we can create custom dies for any unique cake dimensions or multi-compartment sets.
