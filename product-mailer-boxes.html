Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I get my mailer boxes printed on both the inside and outside?

Yes, we offer full-color printing on both the exterior and interior of the box. Double-sided printing is a great way to surprise your customers and enhance the unboxing experience.

What is the most popular material for subscription boxes?

E-flute corrugated cardboard is the standard for subscription and mailer boxes. It provides excellent crush resistance while remaining lightweight for shipping.

Do you provide design support?

Absolutely. iColorPacks provides free structural dieline files and 3D mockups within 24 hours to help you visualize your design before production.
