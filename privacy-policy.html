Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how Wenzhou iColor Packaging Co., Ltd. (iColorPacks) uses information when you browse www.icolorpacks.com or contact us about packaging.

Information you provide

When you request a quotation, we may receive your name, email or WhatsApp number, company details, packaging specifications, artwork, delivery destination and messages. Provide only information relevant to your project. Do not send payment card details or other sensitive personal information through the inquiry form.

How we use it

We use inquiry information to reply, prepare quotations and samples, discuss artwork and production, arrange agreed delivery and maintain the related business records. Submitting an inquiry asks us to contact you about that project; it does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

Service providers and contact channels

Website inquiry forms are processed through Formspree. WhatsApp links open a conversation through WhatsApp, and email links use your chosen email service. These services process data according to their own policies. Information needed to fulfill an agreed order may also be shared with the relevant production or delivery providers. We do not sell your inquiry information.

Attachments can be sent separately by email or WhatsApp using the same name and project details. The website form does not currently accept file uploads.

Website analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand page visits and interactions such as WhatsApp clicks and successful inquiry submissions. Google Analytics may use cookies and process device, browser and usage information. Our event tracking does not intentionally send your inquiry message, email address or WhatsApp number to Google Analytics. You can control cookies in your browser and use Google's analytics opt-out tools.

Retention and international processing

We retain inquiry and order information for the business purpose described above and applicable record-keeping requirements. Our company is based in China, and service providers may process information in other countries. Contact us if you want to discuss information retained about you or request access, correction or deletion, subject to relevant obligations.
