Expertise

Common Questions.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
At iColorPack, we support both growth-stage and established brands. Our standard MOQ starts at 100 units for luxury rigid boxes and premium shopping bags, ensuring high-end manufacturing is accessible for agile business models.
Can I get a physical sample before bulk production?
Yes. We offer rapid prototyping. A physical sample usually takes 3-5 business days. While there is a nominal fee for sampling, this cost is fully credited back to your account upon confirmation of your bulk order.
How long does the production process take?
Our standard production cycle is 15 days from the date of final design approval. For urgent global campaigns, we offer expedited manufacturing slots. Total turnaround time depends on the specific complexity and volume of the project.
Do you provide technical dielines and 3D mockups?
Our engineering team provides full technical support. We generate professional dielines and high-fidelity 3D mockups within 24 hours to help you visualize the final product and ensure perfect alignment with your branding assets.
What printing and finishing techniques do you offer?
As a premier manufacturer, we offer a complete range of finishes: Gold/Silver hot stamping, multi-level embossing, spot UV, soft-touch matte lamination, and specialty textured paper mounting. All processes are handled in-house for maximum quality control.
Do you ship to Amazon FBA or international warehouses?
Yes. We have a dedicated logistics team experienced in direct FBA delivery, including specialized labeling, palletization, and customs clearance for the US, Europe, and Asia-Pacific markets.
