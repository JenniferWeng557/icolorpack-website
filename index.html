The details that make it yours

Choose the finish. Change the impression.

Start with the product, the paper and the way the pack will be handled. Combine finishes deliberately rather than adding every option.

✨

Foil stamping

Add a metallic highlight to a logo, border or short message. Review fine lines, small type and placement on the chosen paper before tooling.

🤚

Soft-touch or matte lamination

Create a low-gloss surface with a different hand feel. Compare scuff appearance, fold behavior and compatibility with your chosen decorative finish.

💎

Spot UV

Use selective gloss to contrast with a quieter background. Clear artwork separation and alignment are important for small logos and tight patterns.

🔠

Embossing & debossing

Raise or recess a mark for a tactile effect. Detail, depth and position depend on the paper and construction; approve the result on a representative sample.

🌈

Full-color printing

Build the design with imagery, patterns or brand colors. Review the selected paper and printing reference because screen colors are not a physical color standard.

🧩

Inserts & interior details

Support the product with paperboard, foam or other reviewed materials. Plan retention, finger access and the reveal alongside the outer box.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
Our minimum order quantity is 100 pieces per size and artwork across all product types. Small-batch orders have a higher unit price because setup costs are spread over fewer pieces. Send your size, material, printing, finish and quantity for a project quotation.
Where is the iColorPacks luxury packaging factory located?
Our team is based in Wenzhou, Zhejiang, China. If you need company or production verification for a sourcing review, include that request with your inquiry.
What information is needed for a useful quote?
Please provide the product or internal dimensions, packed weight, quantity by design, preferred structure and material, artwork status, finishes, insert needs, destination and target arrival date.
How are sampling, production and delivery times confirmed?
Timing is reviewed after the structure, materials, finish, quantity, artwork status, sample route and destination are known. Sampling, production and freight should be confirmed as separate stages.

Paper swatches and existing samples from our sample room are free for reviewing styles and quality. You only pay the shipping cost. Existing samples do not include new custom branding.

Custom samples

Custom samples require a sample fee and shipping charge. The sample fee is refundable after you place the bulk production order. Sample shipping is paid separately and is not included in the sample-fee refund. The sample specification, fee and refund arrangement are recorded in your quotation.
