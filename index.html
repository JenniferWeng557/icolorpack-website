Our Story

Born in Wenzhou.
Built for Brands.

"

In 2008, we started with a simple belief: great packaging is not just a box — it is the first impression your brand makes. In a small workshop in Wenzhou, China's packaging capital, we began crafting paper bags and gift boxes by hand.

Over 15 years, that workshop grew into a full-scale factory with 200+ skilled craftspeople and ISO 9001 certified quality systems. But our belief hasn't changed. We still obsess over every fold, every foil, and every fiber.

Today, we serve hundreds of global brands who trust us to turn their packaging vision into a physical reality that their customers love to unbox.
