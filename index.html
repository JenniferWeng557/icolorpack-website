Our Story

Born in Wenzhou.
Built for Brands.

"

In 2008, we started with a simple belief: great packaging is not just a box — it is the first impression your brand makes. In a small workshop in Wenzhou, China's packaging capital, we began crafting paper bags and gift boxes by hand.

Over 15 years, that workshop grew into a full-scale factory with 200+ skilled craftspeople and ISO 9001 certified quality systems. But our belief never changed — every piece we make must make the person who receives it pause and feel something.

Today, we serve 500+ brands and distributors across Europe, North America and beyond. When you work with iColorPack, you are not just buying packaging — you are partnering with a team that cares about your brand as much as you do.

Jennifer
Export Director · Wenzhou iColor Packaging Co.
