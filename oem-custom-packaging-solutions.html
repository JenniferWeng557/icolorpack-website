Buyer questions

OEM packaging FAQ

What should I send first?

Send product dimensions and weight, target quantity, destination, artwork or brand references, preferred packaging style and launch date. Photos and physical samples help when the structure must fit an existing product.

Can you recommend a lower-cost structure?

Yes. We can compare structures, materials, inserts and finishing combinations while protecting the most important presentation and performance goals.

Can I see a sample before production?

Yes. We will identify whether a structural sample, printed sample or both are needed for fit, appearance and opening-sequence approval.
