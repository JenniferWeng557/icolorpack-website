Common Pain Points in Cosmetic Packaging

Surface Protection

Premium cosmetics need scratch-resistant finishes to maintain a flawless look from the factory to the customer's vanity.

Tactile Experience

Standard packaging often feels "cheap." Beauty brands require soft-touch textures and weighted structures to convey luxury.

New Launch Testing

Startups and established brands alike need low MOQ options to test new product lines without heavy inventory risk.

Our Signature Solutions

Luxury Magnetic Rigid Boxes

Perfect for skincare sets and perfume. Our magnetic boxes offer a satisfying "click" upon closing and a premium rigid structure that protects fragile glass containers.

Branded Paper Shopping Bags

Complete the retail experience with high-GSM cardboard bags featuring ribbon handles and gold foil logos. Ideal for boutique checkout and gift-giving.

Materials & Craftsmanship

Soft-Touch Lamination

Provides a velvet-like feel to the surface, making the box irresistible to touch while adding a layer of scratch resistance.

Hot Foil Stamping

Custom EVA/Foam Inserts

Cosmetic Packaging FAQ

What is the MOQ for custom cosmetic boxes?
Our typical MOQ for luxury rigid boxes starts at 500 units, while folding cardboard boxes can start at 1,000 units. We offer flexibility for emerging brands.
Can you match specific Pantone colors?
Yes, we use Heidelberg offset printing to ensure 95%+ accuracy for your brand's specific Pantone colors.
Do you provide samples before mass production?
Absolutely. We offer digital samples, 3D mockups, and physical pre-production samples to ensure every detail is perfect.
