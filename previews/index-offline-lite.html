Plan the Right Specification

What We Review Before Quoting.

Perfume & Fragile Products

Protection and Insert Fit

Provide: Bottle dimensions, packed weight, cap clearance and destination.

Review: Box structure, board, insert material, cavity fit and protective packing.

Approve: Product fit, removal, surface protection and handling on the finished sample.

Boutique Paper Bags

Carrying Strength and Finish

Provide: Packed product size, weight, desired handle and brand artwork.

Review: Paper stock, top and base reinforcement, handle attachment, print and finish.

Approve: Carry test, handle comfort, color and foil or lamination on the finished sample.

E-commerce Mailers

Protection and Shipping Volume

Provide: Product dimensions, weight, order quantity and fulfillment method.

Review: Internal size, flute, locking tabs, print coverage, insert and outer-carton packing.

Approve: Packing efficiency, product movement, closure and transport-test requirements.

A More Useful First Reply

Prepare Your RFQ.

1. Product and Size

Share the product dimensions, packed weight and required internal or finished packaging size.

2. Quantity and Destination

State the quantity for each design, delivery country or postcode, and your preferred trade term if known.

3. Structure and Material

Describe the preferred box or bag style, board or paper, insert and any reference sample you want us to review.

4. Artwork and Finish

Attach artwork when available and identify Pantone colors, foil, embossing, UV, lamination or other finish requirements.

5. Sample Route

Tell us whether you need a structural blank, printed sample or production-reference sample before bulk approval.

6. Target Date

Share the required arrival date. Sampling, production and freight timing can then be reviewed separately.

Common Questions.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
The minimum order for paper bags and cake boxes is 500 pieces. Send the quantity and requirements for each design so we can review your project.
Where is the iColorPacks luxury packaging factory located?
Our team is based in Wenzhou, Zhejiang, China. If you need company or production verification for a sourcing review, include that request with your inquiry.
What information is needed for a useful quote?
Please provide the product or internal dimensions, packed weight, quantity by design, preferred structure and material, artwork status, finishes, insert needs, destination and target arrival date.
How are sampling, production and delivery times confirmed?
Paper bag and cake box sampling normally takes 3–5 days. Production normally takes 10–15 days after sample approval. Freight time is separate and depends on your destination and shipping method; confirm the final schedule with your quotation.
