Jewelry Industry Challenges

The "Luxury Gap"

Cheap packaging can diminish the perceived value of high-end jewelry. Consistency in brand quality is key.

Delicate Protection

Items like necklaces and earrings need secure, soft-lined compartments to prevent tangling and scratches during transit.

Anti-Tarnish Storage

Using acid-free papers and quality materials to ensure the jewelry remains bright and untarnished over time.

Premium Jewelry Structures

Elegant Drawer Boxes

Smooth-sliding rigid boxes with ribbon pulls. A modern classic for rings, earrings, and necklaces.

Two-Piece Lid Boxes

The traditional luxury choice. Available in various depths and custom sizes to fit any jewelry piece perfectly.

Artisanal Finishing

Velvet & Microfiber

High-quality inserts that provide a soft bed for your products while enhancing the "expensive" feel.

Hot Foil Stamping

Classic gold or silver foil on textured paper creates a high-contrast, premium brand identity.

UV Spot Gloss

Add a subtle, reflective layer to specific parts of your design for a sophisticated, modern look.

Jewelry Packaging FAQ

What materials do you use for jewelry boxes?
We use premium gray board (1200gsm+) wrapped in specialty art papers, textured papers, or pearlized paper for a luxury finish.
Can you provide custom inserts?
Yes, we offer custom-cut EVA, sponge with velvet coating, and microfiber pouches to secure and display your jewelry.
