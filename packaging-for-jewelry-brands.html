1. Core Answers for Jewelry Sourcing (Answer First)

Premium jewelry brands should prioritize 1200g Industrial Paperboard wrapped with specialty art paper for a high-end feel. In the China market, MOQs typically follow three tiers: 100pcs (digital sampling/market test), 500pcs (standard production MOQ), and 2000pcs+ (optimized unit cost for volume production). iColorPacks recommends Drawer Boxes or Lid-and-Base styles to balance aesthetics with manufacturing cost.

3. iColorPacks MOQ & Cost Tiers

As a specialized packaging manufacturer, iColorPacks offers three flexible sourcing solutions for jewelry brands:

  • Micro-batch Testing (100-200pcs): Ideal for new product launches. Higher unit price, but eliminates the risk of carrying thousands of units in stock.
  • Brand Growth Stage (500-1000pcs): Best value for money. Supports full premium finishes including Gold Foil, Spot UV, and Embossing.
  • Volume Production (2000pcs+): Best possible unit price, suitable for established bestsellers.

5. FAQ: 5 Common Questions About Jewelry Packaging

Q1: Why does my jewelry box have a strong odor?

A: Low-quality glue is the culprit. iColorPacks uses eco-friendly water-based glue to ensure odorless packaging that protects precious metals from corrosion.

Q2: Will the gold foil flake or peel off?

A: We use imported Kurz foil. When correctly matched with paper texture and sufficient pressure, the foil provides exceptional adhesion.

Q3: Do you offer sustainable/eco-friendly options?

A: Yes. We provide FSC-certified papers, plastic-free coatings, and soy-based ink printing to meet high-end sustainability standards.
