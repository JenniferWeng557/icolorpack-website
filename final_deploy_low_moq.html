1. Why Choose Low MOQ Sourcing? (Answer First)

For B2B brands, Low MOQ (typically 100-500 units) is key to **Market Testing** and **Inventory Control**. it allows brands to launch "limited editions" or "social media exclusives" with minimal risk—a need that iColorPacks is specifically equipped to serve as a specialized China factory.

4. Digital Printing vs. Traditional Offset: Which One?

- **< 300pcs**: Highly recommend **Digital Printing**. 2-3 days sampling.
- **> 500pcs**: Recommend returning to **Offset Printing** for better unit cost.
