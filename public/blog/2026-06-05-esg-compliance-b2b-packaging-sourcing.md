In 2026, the primary risk for a B2B packaging buyer has shifted from "can I get it on time?" to "can I prove how it was made?". 

With the full implementation of the **Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)** in the EU and similar supply chain transparency mandates in the US and UK, B2B buyers are now legally responsible for the ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) performance of their suppliers. If your packaging partner cannot provide a clear audit trail, your brand faces significant regulatory fines, customs delays, and irreparable damage to consumer trust.
