Common Questions.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
We offer agile MOQ starting at 100-500 units depending on the complexity and design.
Do you provide free design support?
Yes, our in-house design team can assist with structural design, logo placement, and technical file optimization.
Can you ship directly to Amazon FBA warehouses?
Absolutely. We handle all labeling, palletizing, and shipping documentation for direct FBA delivery globally.
What are your typical production lead times?
Standard production takes 12-18 days. Urgent orders can be expedited depending on current factory capacity.
What shipping methods do you offer?
We offer sea freight (economical), air freight (fast), and door-to-door DDP services for most countries.
Are your materials FSC certified?
Yes, we prioritize sustainability and can provide FSC-certified paper and biodegradable materials upon request.
What custom finishes can you apply?
We offer gold/silver foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, matte/gloss lamination, and unique textures.
