Professional Custom Rigid Box Styles

Choosing the right box structure is critical for unboxing experience and shipping efficiency. We offer 4 main rigid box types for global B2B procurement. For MOQ, material, finishing and logistics cost factors, see our custom packaging cost guide.

Lid & Base Box

The classic luxury standard. Top and bottom separate completely. Ideal for iPhones, high-end electronics, and gift sets.

Magnetic Closure

Features hidden magnets for a satisfying 'click'. Premium choice for jewelry, perfume, and cosmetics.

Sliding Drawer

A modern, sleek opening mechanism. Perfect for boutique items, stationery, and small luxury goods.

Shoulder & Neck

Includes an internal 'neck' that holds the lid slightly apart for a layered, high-contrast look.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical lead time for custom rigid boxes?

Our standard production time is 12-18 business days after sample approval. Air shipping takes 5-7 days, while DDP ocean shipping takes 25-35 days.

Can I get a physical sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We provide 48h physical sampling. If you provide your artwork, we can ship a production-grade sample within 3-5 days.

Do you offer design support?

Absolutely. We provide free 3D mockups and structural design support for all wholesale orders.
