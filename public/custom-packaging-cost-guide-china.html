Cost Drivers

What affects custom packaging cost?

The biggest cost drivers are structure, quantity, material, printing and finishing. A simple kraft paper bag and a rigid gift box may both be custom packaging, but they do not use the same board, labor, assembly process or shipping volume.

Cost factorHow it affects priceBuyer note
Box or bag typeRigid boxes usually require more board, handwork and assembly than folding cartons or paper bags.Choose structure based on product value, shipping risk and brand positioning.
Order quantityUnit cost usually decreases as quantity increases because setup and printing costs are spread across more units.Very small orders are possible, but the unit cost will be higher.
MaterialGrayboard, kraft paper, art paper, ivory board, corrugated board and specialty paper have different costs.Match material to product weight, retail positioning and sustainability goals.
Paper thickness / GSMHigher GSM or thicker board improves strength and feel but increases material cost and shipping weight.Use higher GSM only where strength or premium feel matters.
Surface finishingFoil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, soft touch and lamination add process steps.Use premium finishing on visible brand areas instead of covering hidden surfaces.
Shipping methodSea, air, express and DDP delivery have different timing and cost structures.Large lightweight boxes are affected by volumetric weight.

Decision Guide

When to choose lower-cost or premium packaging

Choose a lower-cost option when...

The product is lightweight, not fragile, highly price-sensitive, or the packaging is mainly for basic protection. Kraft paper bags, simple paper bags, folding cartons and corrugated mailer boxes can be practical starting points.

Choose premium packaging when...

The product is jewelry, perfume, skincare, candles or a luxury gift. Rigid boxes, magnetic closure boxes, drawer boxes, specialty paper, foil stamping and custom inserts can create stronger perceived value and better protection.

Why are rigid boxes more expensive than paper bags or folding cartons?

Rigid boxes usually use thicker grayboard, more hand assembly, more complex structure and more premium finishing. They are designed for stronger protection and a higher-end gifting experience.

What is the cheapest custom packaging option?

For many projects, simple kraft paper bags, basic paper bags or corrugated mailer boxes are more cost-efficient than rigid gift boxes. The best option depends on product weight, brand positioning and shipping risk.

Does foil stamping increase packaging cost?

Yes. Foil stamping adds an additional process and setup step. To control cost, use foil stamping on key brand areas such as the logo or front panel.
