Frequently Asked Questions

Ordering & MOQ

Minimums, pricing and how to start your custom packaging project.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on the packaging structure, dimensions, material, printing and finish. Share your specification and target quantity so we can confirm the practical minimum for your project.

How is the price of my custom packaging calculated?

Your final cost is determined by size, material, structure, insert, print coverage, surface finishes, packing method, order volume and delivery term. Compare quotations using the same specification.

Can I get a custom sample before bulk production?

Yes, we provide physical samples or 3D digital mockups to ensure the design, material, and printing meet your expectations before starting bulk manufacturing.

Design & Samples

Free design support, dielines and mockups from our in-house team.

Do you provide free packaging design support?

Design support can include structural review, dieline guidance, logo placement and a digital mockup. Scope and timing are confirmed after we review your product dimensions, artwork and project requirements.

Can I get my boxes printed on both the inside and outside?

Yes, we offer full-color printing on both the exterior and interior of the box. Double-sided printing is a great way to surprise your customers and enhance the unboxing experience.

What materials are available for custom boxes and bags?

We work with rigid board, kraft, and ivory board options, plus E-flute and B-flute corrugated cardboard for mailer boxes. Finishes include matte/glossy lamination, soft touch, UV coating, gold/silver hot stamping and embossing.

Production & Shipping

Lead times and global delivery, including direct-to-FBA.

What are your typical production lead times?

Sampling and production time depend on the structure, materials, finishes, quantity and approval rounds. We confirm sampling, production and freight timing separately against your required arrival date.

Can you ship directly to Amazon FBA warehouses?

Direct-to-warehouse and DDP options can be reviewed for your destination. Availability, labeling, documentation and delivery terms are confirmed for each order before production.

Where is the iColorPacks factory located?

Our facility is located in Wenzhou, China, known globally as the hub for premium B2B packaging and printing excellence.

Still have questions?

Send your size, quantity, artwork status, destination and target date for a specification-based reply.
