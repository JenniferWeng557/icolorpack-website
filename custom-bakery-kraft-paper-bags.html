Planning your bakery kraft paper bags

📐

Start with the packed food, not the bag size

Measure the loaf, pastry tray or takeaway carton as it will actually be packed. Tell us whether food touches the paper and whether warmth, oil or moisture is expected.

✓

Test the real bakery routine

Load the intended contents and check base support, handle comfort and surface marking. Food-contact suitability depends on the selected paper, inks, barriers and destination requirements.

Customization & approval

Choose kraft packaging by use, not by appearance alone

📐

Dimensions and filled weight

Specify front width, gusset and body height. Include the packed item dimensions, maximum load and whether the bag must accommodate a tray or retail carton.

🌿

Paper and print choices

Confirm brown or white kraft, print coverage and reinforcement. Recycled content, recyclability and food-contact claims require material-specific evidence; they are not automatic properties of every kraft bag.

🛍️

Carrying and contact checks

Test the handles, patches, bottom folds and base with representative contents. For food use, identify direct contact, moisture and grease exposure so suitable materials can be reviewed.

Questions before you order

Bakery kraft paper bags: buying questions

Will artwork look the same on brown and white kraft?

Not necessarily. The paper shade changes the appearance of inks. Use a physical print reference where color matching matters.

Can every kraft bag be used for food?

No. Specify whether the food is wrapped or touches the paper and identify destination requirements. Suitability depends on the full material and printing specification.
