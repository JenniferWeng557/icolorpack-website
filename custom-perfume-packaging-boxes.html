Packaging engineering

Protection, alignment and reveal in one system

Perfume packaging must manage a dense glass bottle while preserving a premium opening experience. The box and insert are therefore specified together.

01 / BOTTLE FIT

Measured support points

The insert can support the base, body, shoulders or cap area according to bottle geometry and removal access.

02 / STRUCTURE

Format matched to the launch

Magnetic, shoulder-neck, lid-and-base and folding-carton routes suit different budgets, weights and retail positions.

03 / BRAND FINISH

Controlled decoration

Buyer questions

Perfume packaging FAQ

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Bottle dimensions, filled weight, quantity, destination, preferred structure, artwork and target date. A physical bottle or dimensioned drawing reduces uncertainty.

How do you stop the bottle moving?

The insert is developed around the bottle geometry and tested for fit. The correct material depends on weight, fragility, sustainability priorities and presentation.

Can I approve a sample before production?

Yes. We identify whether a structural sample, printed sample or both are appropriate for the decisions you need to verify.
