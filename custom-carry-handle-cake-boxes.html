The answer depends on board grade, dimensions and handle geometry; we validate the final design with load testing.

Can I use an insert for six or twelve cupcakes?

Yes. Insert layouts are customized to the cupcake diameter and count.

Can the box ship flat?

Many carry-handle designs can ship flat and be assembled at the bakery.
