Low MOQ Custom Packaging

1. When does low MOQ custom packaging make sense?

Low MOQ custom packaging can help a growing brand test a product, seasonal design or new market without committing to unnecessary inventory. The practical minimum depends on the box or bag structure, dimensions, board or paper availability, printing method, finishes and artwork versions. A quantity near 500 pieces is a common starting point for many paper-packaging projects, but the factory must confirm the actual MOQ after reviewing the complete specification.

3. How we review a small-batch packaging request

We first check the internal size, structure, board or paper, print coverage, finishes, insert requirements and target quantity. The quotation should separate tooling, sampling, unit production and shipping so buyers can compare the total project cost. Certification or sustainability documentation is supplied only when it is available for the selected material and required by the destination market.

4. Digital printing vs. offset printing

Digital printing can suit short runs, variable artwork and projects where avoiding plates matters. Offset printing can suit repeat production, demanding color control and quantities that justify setup. The correct choice depends on substrate, print area, finish, approved color standard and order quantity; lead time and unit-price differences should be confirmed in the written quotation.

5. FAQ: What B2B Buyers Ask Most

Q: Can a small-batch order use foil, UV or embossing?
A: These finishes may be possible, but each one can add tooling, setup or material requirements. Confirm feasibility and MOQ against the selected structure, artwork and quantity.
Q: Can the sampling fee be credited towards my bulk order?
A: Sample charges and any credit arrangement are confirmed in the written quotation before payment.
Q: Is air, courier or sea freight better for a small order?
A: Compare packed volume and weight, destination, required arrival date, customs terms and handling risk. The best route can only be selected after the packed shipment data is available.
Q: What information is needed to confirm MOQ?

A: Send the internal size, structure, material, print coverage, finishes, insert requirements, artwork versions and destination. MOQ is confirmed after these specifications are reviewed.
