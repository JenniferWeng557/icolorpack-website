Engineering priorities

The quality is felt in the pull

A drawer box can look excellent and still fail if the tray binds, drops or twists. We focus on the tolerances that make the opening movement consistent after wrapping and packing.

01 / CLEARANCE

Smooth sleeve-to-tray fit

Finished paper thickness and dimensional tolerance are considered so the tray slides without an uncontrolled gap.

02 / OPENING

Purposeful pull detail

Ribbon, fabric tab, paper tab or finger notch is selected for the tray weight, box size and desired visual style.

03 / PRESENTATION

Organized product reveal

A fitted platform or cavity positions the product as the drawer opens and limits movement in distribution.

Buyer questions

Drawer box FAQ

How do you avoid a drawer that is too tight?

We account for the completed thickness of the wrapped sleeve and tray, then confirm the opening action during sampling. The correct clearance depends on size, wrap and climate exposure.

Which pull style is most durable?

Durability depends on tray weight and attachment method. Ribbon and fabric tabs work well in many premium applications, while finger notches avoid an added component. We recommend one after reviewing the load.

Can one insert hold several products?

Yes. Provide the dimensions and weights of every item plus the preferred orientation. We can evaluate support points, spacing and removal access.

Can I approve the opening action before production?

Yes. A structural sample is especially useful for testing sleeve clearance, pull placement and product fit before artwork-heavy production begins.

Your product, your specification

Planning your drawer rigid boxes

Use the product you actually intend to pack as the starting point. These checks help turn a visual reference into an approvable packaging specification.

📐

Specify usable tray space

Measure the product arrangement inside the tray, then allow room for the insert and finger access. The outer sleeve size is not the same as usable internal space.

✓

Balance smooth opening with secure retention

Review sleeve-to-tray clearance, pull attachment and stops where required. Test the loaded drawer repeatedly so a smooth opening does not become an unintended slide-out.

Customization & approval

Build a specification that can be approved

📐

Internal size and clearance

State usable internal length × width × height, product weight and the arrangement of every component. Include the insert and the space needed to lift the product out.

✨

Paper, print and finishing

Compare wrapping paper, printed color, foil, embossing and spot UV on physical references where possible. Fine details, deep colors and textured papers need artwork review.

🧩

Insert and closure approval

Choose paperboard, foam or fabric-covered support according to the item. Approve the fitted pack, opening force, visible seams and surface protection before bulk production.
