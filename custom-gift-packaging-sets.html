Buyer questions

Gift packaging set FAQ

Can you supply the complete packaging set?

Yes. We coordinate gift boxes with branded tissue paper, stickers or gold seals, hang tags, greeting and message cards, ribbon and matching wrapping paper. Select only the components you need; each item is listed separately in the quotation.

Can the accessories match my box and logo?

Yes. Share your logo file, color references and finish preferences. We review artwork, print and foil positions across the set. Different papers and ribbons can reproduce color differently, so approve physical samples before production.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Custom gift boxes start at 100 pieces per size and artwork. Tell us the quantities for each accessory; its specification and minimum are confirmed separately before you order.

Can I review samples before ordering in bulk?

Yes. Existing reference samples are free with shipping charged separately. Custom-branded samples require a sample fee and shipping; the quotation records the sample specification and refund arrangement.

Can sets arrive packed together?

Tell us whether you need loose components for local fulfillment or pre-assembled packaging kits. We review the component count, folding, placement, protection and carton labeling, then confirm assembly charges and shipment volume in the quotation.

Reply within 1 hour · Quotation within 24 hours. Share the size, quantity, artwork and delivery destination for your quotation.

Free reference samples

Paper swatches and existing samples from our sample room are free for reviewing styles and quality. You only pay the shipping cost. Existing samples do not include new custom branding.

Custom samples

Custom samples require a sample fee and shipping charge. The sample fee is refundable after you place the bulk production order. Sample shipping is paid separately and is not included in the sample-fee refund. The sample specification, fee and refund arrangement are recorded in your quotation.

Order & payment

Confirm the specification and quotation, review the sample, approve the artwork and then proceed to bulk production. Payment method, currency, deposit and balance schedule are confirmed in the written order before payment. Use only the payment details confirmed with Jennifer.

Production & shipping

Sample-making, bulk production and transit are scheduled separately. The production start date follows the agreed approval and payment conditions. Sea, train, air and express options can be reviewed for your destination. FOB, CIF, DAP or DDP availability and included charges are confirmed in the quotation.
