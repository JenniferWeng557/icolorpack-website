BEFORE YOU ORDER

Gift packaging set questions

Do I need to order every item in the picture?

No. Choose boxes only, accessories only, or a coordinated selection. The image is a concept reference, and the quoted component list defines what your order includes.

Can all components use the same logo and colors?

Yes, we can coordinate the artwork and color direction. Paper, ribbon and metallic finishes have different surfaces, so exact appearance must be reviewed on suitable physical references.

Does tissue paper make the box moisture-proof?

Ordinary wrapping tissue is used for presentation and surface protection. If you need a moisture barrier, specify the product and storage conditions so we can review a suitable material separately.

What is the minimum order and how is a set priced?

Custom boxes start at 100 pieces per size and artwork. Accessory quantities and production minimums depend on the selected specification and are confirmed separately. The quotation lists the chosen components and extra charges rather than assuming a universal price per set.

Can you pack the accessories with the boxes?

We can review coordinated supply, assembly and kitting for your project. Confirm the packing sequence, component count, protective packing and any assembly charge before production. Final lead times and shipping are agreed in the quotation.
