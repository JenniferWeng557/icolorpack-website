Frequently Asked

What is the lowest MOQ you offer?

We support low-MOQ orders starting from 100 units for select paper bag and mailer box products, and from 500 units for rigid boxes, so early-stage brands can order custom packaging without huge upfront volume.

Is low MOQ packaging more expensive per unit?

Low MOQ runs cost more per unit than bulk orders due to setup efficiency, but we keep entry pricing accessible for startups, and unit costs typically drop 40% once you reorder at 1,000+ units.

Can I get a sample before committing?

Yes, we provide 48-hour physical sampling, and if you supply your artwork we can ship a production-grade sample within 3-5 days before you place your full order.

Do low MOQ orders still get free design support?

Yes. All orders, regardless of quantity, include free structural design assistance and a 3D mockup within 24 hours.
