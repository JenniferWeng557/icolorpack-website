Specification guide

Start with the confection dimensions

Provide the longest width, depth and height for each piece, the assortment count and the total filled weight. Temperature, grease and direct-contact needs should be stated explicitly.

Buyer questions

Chocolate box FAQ

Can different chocolate shapes share one tray?

Yes, when exact dimensions and the intended arrangement are supplied. We review spacing, removal access and support for each shape.

Can I use one box with seasonal sleeves?

Potentially. The sleeve must match the finished box tolerance, and order quantities for each printed version must be reviewed.

How do you confirm food suitability?

Tell us whether packaging will touch the product directly, the intended market and required documentation. Materials are then evaluated against the stated requirements.
