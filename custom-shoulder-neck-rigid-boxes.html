Buyer Questions

Before sampling

Yes. The shoulder can use a different wrap, metallic paper or printed pattern to create a visible reveal.

How do you keep the lid aligned?

We tune the neck height, clearance and board thickness against a physical prototype before mass production.

Can you make inserts for several products?

Yes. We can divide the interior for bottles, accessories, cards and other components.

Your product, your specification

Planning your shoulder-neck rigid boxes

Use the product you actually intend to pack as the starting point. These checks help turn a visual reference into an approvable packaging specification.

📐

Define the shoulder reveal

Specify the visible band between lid and base, the desired lid depth and internal product clearance. The shoulder is both a visual detail and part of the lid alignment.

✓

Check flushness and repeated closure

Review the neck covering, corner seams and lid fit with the finished paper. Confirm that the insert does not prevent the lid from seating evenly.

Customization & approval

Build a specification that can be approved

📐

Internal size and clearance

State usable internal length × width × height, product weight and the arrangement of every component. Include the insert and the space needed to lift the product out.

✨

Paper, print and finishing

Compare wrapping paper, printed color, foil, embossing and spot UV on physical references where possible. Fine details, deep colors and textured papers need artwork review.

🧩

Insert and closure approval

Choose paperboard, foam or fabric-covered support according to the item. Approve the fitted pack, opening force, visible seams and surface protection before bulk production.

Questions before you order

Shoulder-neck rigid boxes: buying questions

Can the outside dimensions match my product dimensions?

Not directly. Board, wrapping and inserts take space. Tell us the product dimensions and whether your requested size is internal or external; we will review a suitable construction.

What should I approve before production?

Confirm the drawing, artwork version, material and finish, then check a representative sample for product fit, opening and appearance. Agree any transport checks separately.

Can you support design and quotation?

Yes. iColorPacks provides free design support and quotations within 24 hours. Send size, quantity, artwork references and destination so the proposal can be based on your actual project.
