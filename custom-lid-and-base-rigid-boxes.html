Construction choices

Simple form, many presentation outcomes

The familiar two-piece format can feel minimal, ceremonial or highly decorative. The difference comes from proportional control, paper selection, lid fit and how the product sits inside.

01 / PROPORTION

Full or partial-depth lid

Lid depth changes the silhouette, opening force and amount of base color visible when the box is closed.

02 / REVEAL

Standard or shoulder-neck

Add a raised inner neck for a contrasting reveal line, improved alignment and a more architectural presentation.

03 / INTERIOR

Insert made for the contents

Product-specific cavities, platforms and pull access can organize one item or a coordinated set.

Buyer questions

Lid and base box FAQ

What is a shoulder-neck rigid box?

It adds a raised inner neck between the base and lid. This can help align the lid and create a visible reveal band, often in a contrasting color or material.

Can the lid depth be changed?

Yes. Full-depth lids create a continuous exterior, while shorter lids expose part of the base. The proportion is chosen with the box dimensions and intended opening feel.

How is the correct insert selected?

We review product size, weight, fragility, removal method and sustainability priorities. No single insert material is correct for every project.

What is needed for an accurate quote?

Internal box size or exact product dimensions, item weight, quantity, destination, artwork and finishing preferences. A photo or sample of the product can reduce ambiguity.
