Buyer questions

Lid and base box FAQ

What is a shoulder-neck rigid box?

It adds a raised inner neck between the base and lid. This can help align the lid and create a visible reveal band, often in a contrasting color or material.

Can the lid depth be changed?

Yes. Full-depth lids create a continuous exterior, while shorter lids expose part of the base. The proportion is chosen with the box dimensions and intended opening feel.

How is the correct insert selected?

We review product size, weight, fragility, removal method and sustainability priorities. No single insert material is correct for every project.

What is needed for an accurate quote?

Internal box size or exact product dimensions, item weight, quantity, destination, artwork and finishing preferences. A photo or sample of the product can reduce ambiguity.

Your product, your specification

Planning your lid-and-base rigid boxes

Use the product you actually intend to pack as the starting point. These checks help turn a visual reference into an approvable packaging specification.

📐

Choose the lid depth and reveal

Confirm how much of the base remains visible and whether the customer lifts the lid or slides it off. Product height, insert height and the closure gap must be considered together.

✓

Check the fit on a finished sample

Covering paper and lining add thickness. Review lifting force, corner finish and repeated closure using the finished materials rather than only an undecorated blank.

Customization & approval

Build a specification that can be approved

📐

Internal size and clearance

State usable internal length × width × height, product weight and the arrangement of every component. Include the insert and the space needed to lift the product out.

✨

Paper, print and finishing

Compare wrapping paper, printed color, foil, embossing and spot UV on physical references where possible. Fine details, deep colors and textured papers need artwork review.

🧩

Insert and closure approval

Choose paperboard, foam or fabric-covered support according to the item. Approve the fitted pack, opening force, visible seams and surface protection before bulk production.
