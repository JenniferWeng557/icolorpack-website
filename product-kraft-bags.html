Kraft Paper Bag Buying Guide

Choose the structure before choosing the artwork

A reliable kraft paper bag specification starts with the packed product, finished size, expected carrying weight and route to market. Paper weight alone does not determine strength: handle attachment, top fold, base board, adhesive, grain direction and carton packing all affect performance. Use the guidance below to prepare a clearer RFQ and compare quotations on the same basis.

Retail & boutique

Prioritize clean color reproduction, comfortable handles and a size that presents apparel, accessories or boxed products without excess movement.

  • Twisted paper, flat paper or cotton handles
  • Reinforced top fold and base as required
  • Inside printing or tissue-paper coordination

Food takeaway

Confirm the food container dimensions, packed weight, ventilation needs and whether the bag may contact grease, condensation or chilled products.

  • Wide base for stable container placement
  • Handle and adhesive reviewed for load
  • Barrier treatments assessed for end-of-life impact

E-commerce & events

Consider flat-pack efficiency, rapid packing, carton quantity and how the bag will look after storage, transport and repeated handling.

  • Machine-made formats for repeatability
  • Compact master-carton planning
  • QR, campaign or seasonal print options

Specification Data

Paper, handle and reinforcement selection

The ranges below are common starting points for discussion, not guaranteed load ratings. Final material and construction should be confirmed through a finished sample and an agreed carrying test using the actual packed product.

ApplicationTypical paper rangeCommon handleSpecification to review
Light retail itemsAbout 80–110 gsm kraftFlat or twisted paperBag dimensions, handle patch, fold depth and packed weight
Boutique apparel & giftsAbout 110–150 gsm kraftTwisted paper, cotton or ribbonTop reinforcement, base board, print coverage and presentation
Food containersAbout 80–130 gsm kraftFlat or twisted paperBase width, container stability, moisture/grease exposure and ventilation
Heavier boxed productsProject-specific heavier kraft or boardReinforced paper or textile handleActual load test, handle anchoring, base reinforcement and carton protection

“GSM” describes paper mass per square metre; it is not a standalone strength guarantee. Recycled-fibre content, fibre direction, humidity, bag proportions and converting quality can change the result.

Printing & Finishing

Build the brand effect around the paper surface

Flexographic printing

Often considered for simpler artwork and larger repeat runs. Confirm spot colors, ink coverage, registration tolerance and how the natural brown substrate changes the perceived color.

Offset or high-detail print

Useful when the design requires finer detail or broader color control. White kraft or a printed white underlayer may be evaluated when brand colors must appear brighter.

Foil, embossing & varnish

Premium accents can be combined with kraft paper, but each added film, foil, laminate or barrier may affect cost, lead time and recyclability. Specify the finish area and desired effect.

Sustainability Review

“Kraft” does not automatically mean the whole bag is recyclable

A paper bag's end-of-life pathway depends on the complete construction and local collection system. Ask for the paper source, recycled-fibre content where relevant, handle material, adhesives, inks, coatings, laminations and reinforcements. A simpler mono-material paper construction can make separation easier, while plastic lamination, windows, non-paper handles or heavy barrier coatings may require different disposal guidance.

Paper source

State whether you need virgin fibre, recycled content or certified sourcing, and request documentation for any certification claim required by your market.

Material compatibility

Review handles, patches, base boards, windows and coatings together rather than evaluating only the outer paper.

Right-size the bag

A correctly sized bag can reduce unnecessary paper, improve carrying stability and lower packed carton volume.

Buyer FAQ

Custom kraft paper bag questions

What information is needed for a kraft paper bag quote?

Send the finished width, height and gusset, expected packed weight, order quantity, paper preference, handle type, print artwork, finish requirements, delivery destination and target date. A reference photo is helpful, but dimensions and load requirements are still needed.

What paper weight should I choose?

Paper weight should be selected together with bag size, product weight, handle construction and reinforcement. Common projects may begin around 80–150 gsm, while large or heavy formats require project-specific review and testing.

Are brown kraft and white kraft printed the same way?

No. Natural brown kraft influences ink color and usually creates a muted, organic appearance. White kraft provides a brighter base for cleaner brand colors. Printed samples or agreed color standards help set expectations.

Which handle is best for a custom kraft bag?

Flat paper handles suit practical takeaway and retail formats; twisted paper handles give a more structured presentation; cotton or ribbon handles can support a premium look. The final choice depends on weight, comfort, appearance, recycling goals and budget.

Can kraft paper bags be used for food takeaway?

They can be designed around takeaway containers, but the specification must account for container size, heat, grease, condensation, ventilation and local food-contact requirements. The paper bag should not be treated as direct food-contact packaging unless specified and documented for that use.

Are custom kraft paper bags recyclable?

Many paper-based constructions may be recyclable where suitable collection and processing exist, but the complete bag matters. Laminations, barrier coatings, windows, textile handles and other components can change the appropriate disposal route.

Can I approve a sample before bulk production?

Yes. Depending on what must be checked, the sample route may include a structural blank, digital mockup, printed prototype or production-reference sample. Confirm what the sample represents before approval.

How are MOQ and lead time confirmed?

MOQ and timing depend on finished size, paper, handle, printing, finishes, quantity, sample route and production schedule. They should be confirmed after the complete specification and required delivery date are reviewed.
