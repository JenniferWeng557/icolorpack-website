# 询盘表单与转化追踪方案

## 一、表单问题

当前 B2B 询盘表单字段偏多，适合强意向客户，但对新访客门槛较高。建议改成两级表单。

## 二、轻量表单字段

第一步只收集：

- Name
- Email or WhatsApp
- Product Type
- Quantity
- Size / Message
- Upload File optional

按钮：
Get My Factory Quote

说明：
Send your size + quantity. We will reply with a price range and material suggestion within 12 hours.

## 三、高意向表单字段

放在报价页或弹窗第二步：

- Company Name
- Country / Market
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Size
- Material
- Finish
- Logo Placement
- Existing design file
- Upload artwork
- Target delivery date
- Shipping method

## 四、CTA 文案

首页：
- Get Factory Quote in 12 Hours
- Send Size + Quantity on WhatsApp
- Get Free Dieline + 3D Mockup

产品页：
- Check MOQ & Price
- Get Custom Quote
- Request Sample
- Ask for DDP Shipping Cost

博客页：
- Need Custom Packaging? Get a Free Quote
- Send Your Design for Cost Estimate

## 五、GA4 转化事件

必须设置：

1. quote_form_submit
2. whatsapp_click
3. email_click
4. file_upload
5. product_inquiry_click
6. sample_request_click
7. quote_button_click
8. phone_click

## 六、Microsoft Clarity

安装 Clarity，用于观察：
- 用户是否滚动到表单
- 用户在哪里退出
- 哪些按钮没人点
- 移动端是否存在遮挡
- 表单是否太复杂

## 七、询盘成功页

表单提交后跳转：
/thank-you

Thank-you 页面内容：
- Thank you, we received your request.
- Our packaging consultant will reply within 12 hours.
- You can also message us on WhatsApp for faster support.
- Recommended next step: prepare logo, size and quantity.

该页面用于 GA4 转化统计。
