## 1. The High Cost of the "Dented Corner"
In 2026, the cost of a damaged package extends far beyond the unit price of the box. B2B buyers must account for the **Total Cost of Failure**:

*   **Inventory Deadstock:** Damaged packaging often renders the product unsellable at full price, forcing heavy discounts that erode brand positioning.
