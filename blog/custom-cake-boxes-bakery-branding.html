The Artisan’s Choice: Why Custom Cake Boxes Define 2026 Bakery Branding

Custom tall cake boxes: measure the board and decorated height

The cake diameter is only one part of the specification. A wider board, buttercream decoration or a tall topper can determine the internal size you need. Record the finished cake as it will actually be packed.

A measurement brief for your box sample

  1. Board footprint: measure the board's maximum width and length, or diameter. Include projecting tabs, ribbons and any support tray.
  2. Total packed height: measure from the underside of the board to the highest decoration or topper that stays attached during transport.
  3. Handling clearance: show where hands need access to place and remove the board. Agree the required side and lid clearance on the drawing instead of assuming a standard allowance.
  4. Loaded weight and route: give the total weight and say whether the cake is collected in person, delivered locally or requires a separate shipping system.

For example, an 8-inch cake sitting on a 10-inch board must be sized around the board and the decorated height. This illustrates the measurement method; it is not a recommended final box size. Confirm the usable internal dimensions on a sample.

Window cake box or carry-handle box?

A window affects the lid layout and visible presentation. A handle changes the load path and must be checked with the intended contents. Test the approved construction with a representative loaded board, including opening, closure, lifting and removal. Keep decorations clear of the lid and window.

A presentation box by itself does not establish suitability for parcel shipping. Tell the supplier about the transport route and review any outer carton, restraint, cushioning and temperature requirements as a complete system.

Window cookie gift boxes need a layout brief

For assorted cookies or pastries, provide the size and count of each item, the proposed arrangement and whether pieces can touch. Include cups, liners and dividers in the layout. Review clearance below the window and product movement on a sample before deciding the box size.

Identify which components directly touch food and describe the intended use, including grease exposure and storage conditions. Ask for applicable material documentation for those components; a decorative finish or clear window alone does not establish food-contact suitability.
