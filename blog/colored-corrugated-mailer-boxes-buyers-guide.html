Colored Corrugated Mailer Boxes: Wholesale Buyer's Guide 2026

Published July 11, 2026 · Reading time: 6 min

If you search Google for "colored mailer boxes" or "corrugated shipping boxes wholesale," you'll find hundreds of suppliers. But how do you actually choose the right one — and avoid boxes that arrive crushed, off-color, or weeks late? This guide covers everything a B2B buyer needs to know.

What Are Colored Corrugated Mailer Boxes?

Colored corrugated mailer boxes are shipping boxes made from corrugated fiberboard with custom-printed colors on the exterior. Unlike plain brown kraft boxes, colored mailers serve double duty: protection during transit + brand presentation at unboxing. They're the most popular packaging choice for DTC ecommerce brands shipping apparel, cosmetics, subscription boxes, and premium consumer goods.

Pro tip: Most ecommerce brands choose E-flute. The smoother surface produces sharper print quality for branding, and the weight savings reduce shipping costs. B-flute is overkill unless your product is heavy or fragile.

Color Options and Printing Methods

There are three ways to add color to corrugated mailer boxes:

  • Flexo printing (1-3 colors): Most economical. Best for simple logos and solid colors. MOQ typically 500+ units.
  • Digital printing (full color): No plate fees, photo-quality output. Perfect for small runs (100-300 units) with complex designs.
  • Litho-lamination: High-end offset printing on a paper sheet, then laminated onto corrugated board. Premium finish, higher cost. Best for luxury brands.

Common Buyer Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Ordering the wrong flute. E-flute for under 2kg, B-flute for heavy items. Wrong choice = crushed boxes or wasted money.
  2. Not requesting a physical sample. Colors look different on screen vs. printed on corrugated. Always get a sample.
  3. Ignoring interior printing. The inside of the box is prime branding real estate. Pattern-printed interiors create "wow" unboxing moments for pennies more.
  4. Forgetting about shipping costs. Corrugated boxes are bulky. DDP shipping from China costs more than the boxes themselves at low quantities. Always get a landed cost quote.
