White Cardboard Paper Bags: Choose Board, Handles and Reinforcement

Updated October 26, 2026 · iColorPacks

A paper bag should fit the retail box, carry its actual weight and reproduce your branding on the selected paper. Start with those requirements before choosing a thicker board or an extra finish. iColorPacks supplies custom paper bags from 100 pieces per size and artwork.

1. Specify the paper rather than relying on a product name

“Cardboard gift bag” is a broad sales description. Ask for the paper grade, GSM, coating and a physical reference. White cardboard paper, coated art paper and white kraft paper are different options; put your chosen stock in the written specification.

  • White cardboard paper: compare stiffness, fold appearance and the printed surface on your selected grade.
  • Coated art paper: review print coverage, lamination and whether the reverse side needs printing.
  • Kraft paper: compare the color and surface with your intended logo treatment before substitution.

Compare 250 gsm and 300 gsm samples with the same dimensions, handles and reinforcement if you are deciding between them. GSM alone does not establish carrying capacity. A larger bag or concentrated load can require a different construction.

2. Measure the opening and the filled contents

State front width × side gusset × body height, with units. Provide the outside dimensions and weight of the item or gift box that goes inside. Allow space for inserting and removing it, plus tissue if required. Confirm clearance on a finished sample rather than ordering a bag with identical nominal dimensions to the contents.

3. Describe the handle attachment precisely

For punched-hole ribbon handles without metal eyelets, write that requirement explicitly. Confirm ribbon material, width, color, hole placement, handle drop and the internal fastening method. Request a close-up of the inside attachment and top reinforcement.

Compare satin ribbon, grosgrain ribbon and cotton cord by appearance and comfort with the intended load. The assembled handle and its attachment must be evaluated together; a ribbon's appearance does not prove finished-bag strength.

4. Inspect the top and base with representative contents

  • Check the reinforcement inside the top fold and the base board.
  • Load the sample with the intended contents and agree a suitable carrying check.
  • Inspect handle holes, internal fastening, glued seams and bottom corners after handling.
  • Check whether the base remains level and the opening allows comfortable packing.

5. Approve finishing on the actual paper

Review matte or gloss lamination, foil size and position, fine lines and crease behavior. A digital image cannot prove foil color or resistance to visible scuffing. If you need a particular environmental claim, request evidence for the complete selected construction, including lamination, handles and adhesives.

6. Request comparable quotations

Ask for 100, 500 and 1,000-piece prices using the same size, paper, handles and artwork. Separate tooling, samples and freight from the unit price, and include your destination postcode. Compare total cost and inventory requirements before choosing the quantity.
