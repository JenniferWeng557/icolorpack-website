How to Choose Colored Corrugated Mailer Boxes for Ecommerce Brands

Quick Answer: Choosing the right colored mailer box involves balancing protection, brand aesthetic, and cost. For 2026, focus on E-flute corrugated board for its slim profile and high strength. iColorPacks provides premium custom colored mailer boxes with low 500pcs MOQs and expert design support to help your brand stand out.

In the competitive world of ecommerce, the first physical touchpoint with your customer is the shipping box. A standard brown box might get the job done, but a colored corrugated mailer box makes a statement. Here's how to choose the right one for your brand in 2026.

1. Material Matters: E-Flute vs. B-Flute

Most ecommerce mailers use corrugated cardboard. For a sleek, high-end look, E-flute is the gold standard. it's thin (approx. 1.5mm) but incredibly strong, providing a smooth surface for high-quality printing. If you're shipping heavier items, B-flute (3mm) offers more cushioning but a slightly bulkier feel.

2. Interior vs. Exterior Printing

The "wow" factor often happens when the box is opened. Consider printing a bold color or a custom pattern on the inside of your mailer box. This unexpected burst of color significantly enhances the unboxing experience and encourages social media sharing.

3. Finishing Touches

To truly elevate your colored shipping boxes, consider these finishes:

  • Matte Lamination: For a sophisticated, non-reflective look.
  • Spot UV: To make your logo pop with a glossy contrast.
  • Foil Stamping: Add a touch of luxury with gold or silver accents.

Conclusion
