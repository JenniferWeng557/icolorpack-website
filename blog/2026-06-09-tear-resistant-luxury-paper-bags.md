## 1. The Physics of Paper Failure: Tensile vs. Tear Strength
To solve the "soggy bag" crisis, packaging buyers must understand the mechanical properties that define bag durability:

*   **Tensile Strength:** The maximum stress a paper can withstand while being stretched. Low-quality recycled bags stretch and sag when carrying heavy items.
*   **Tear Resistance:** The resistance of the paper to a tear propagating from a cut or edge. Once a small nick occurs on the top edge of a cheap paper bag, it will rapidly tear down the side.
*   **Moisture Vulnerability:** Standard uncoated paper rapidly loses its hydrogen bonds when exposed to humidity, reducing its burst strength by up to 60%.
