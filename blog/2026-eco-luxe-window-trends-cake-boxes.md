## The Evolution of the Cake Box Window

Traditionally, high-transparency windows were achieved using petroleum-based PET or PVC plastics. While effective for display, these materials are increasingly rejected by eco-conscious consumers. The new standard for the luxury **cake box** involves advanced bio-polymers that offer crystal-clear visibility with a fraction of the environmental footprint.

### 1. PLA (Polylactic Acid) High-Clarity Windows
Derived from renewable resources like corn starch, modern PLA windows have overcome previous issues with "cloudiness." Today's luxury **custom cake box** designs utilize high-grade PLA that rivals the clarity of glass. This allows for an unfiltered view of the product while remaining industrially compostable.

### 2. Cellulose-Based Film Technology
For bakeries seeking 100% plastic-free certifications, cellulose-based films (often derived from wood pulp) are becoming the preferred choice. These films offer excellent moisture-barrier properties—critical for maintaining the structural integrity of the cake—while being home-compostable.
