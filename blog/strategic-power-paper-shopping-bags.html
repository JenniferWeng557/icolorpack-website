For B2B buyers—ranging from boutique owners to global distributors—selecting the right carrier is a critical decision that impacts customer perception and brand recall.

1. The Psychology of the "Premium Carry"

Research into consumer behavior in 2026 shows that the tactile quality of a paper shopping bag directly correlates with the perceived value of the product inside. At iColorPack, we focus on:
