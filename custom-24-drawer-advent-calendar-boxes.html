Count the contents before sizing the cabinet

List every item by day, including its packed dimensions and weight. Equal-looking drawers do not guarantee equal usable space: board thickness, finger access and ribbon pulls also need room.

Questions before you order

24-drawer advent calendar boxes: buying questions

Must every compartment have the same dimensions?

No. A compartment plan can be reviewed around your assortment. Confirm the full day-by-day product list before the structure and artwork are finalized.

Can a visual mockup replace a filled sample?

No. It can help discuss the look, but it does not verify usable space, drawer travel, retention or product access. Approve the physical fit separately.

Can you help design our calendar?

Yes. Free design support and quotations within 24 hours are available. Share the contents map, quantity, branding references and required arrival date; sampling and delivery timings are agreed separately.
