Buyer questions

Jewelry box FAQ

Can I create several box sizes in one collection?

Yes. Send the product list and quantity for each size. We can coordinate the visual system while quoting each construction accurately.

What is needed to design the insert?

Product dimensions, display direction, removal method and photos or samples. For necklaces and earrings, chain or post management should also be explained.

Can bags and cards match the boxes?

Yes. We can coordinate paper, color and logo treatment, while confirming the manufacturing specification and MOQ for each item.
