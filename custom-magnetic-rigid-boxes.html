Buyer questions

Magnetic rigid box FAQ

What information is needed for an accurate quote?

Internal dimensions, product weight, quantity, delivery country, artwork and preferred insert are the best starting points. Photos or a product sample also help us evaluate fit.

Can I approve a sample before production?

Yes. Sampling and artwork confirmation are available before bulk production. The sample route depends on whether you need structural testing, print-color review or both.

Which insert material should I use?

That depends on product weight, fragility, sustainability goals and the intended unboxing experience. We compare paperboard, corrugated, molded pulp, foam and wrapped inserts against those requirements.

Can these boxes ship flat?

Yes, if a collapsible magnetic structure suits the size and use case. We compare freight savings with assembly time and finished-box stiffness before recommending it.
