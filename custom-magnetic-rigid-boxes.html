Buyer questions

Magnetic rigid box FAQ

What information is needed for an accurate quote?

Internal dimensions, product weight, quantity, delivery country, artwork and preferred insert are the best starting points. Photos or a product sample also help us evaluate fit.

Can I approve a sample before production?

Yes. Sampling and artwork confirmation are available before bulk production. The sample route depends on whether you need structural testing, print-color review or both.

Which insert material should I use?

That depends on product weight, fragility, sustainability goals and the intended unboxing experience. We compare paperboard, corrugated, molded pulp, foam and wrapped inserts against those requirements.

Can these boxes ship flat?

Yes, if a collapsible magnetic structure suits the size and use case. We compare freight savings with assembly time and finished-box stiffness before recommending it.

Your product, your specification

Planning your magnetic rigid boxes

Use the product you actually intend to pack as the starting point. These checks help turn a visual reference into an approvable packaging specification.

📐

Select assembled or fold-flat construction

Share storage limits, shipment volume and the packed product size. Fold-flat and assembled boxes differ in packing method, setup steps and how the walls are supported.

✓

Check the closure with the product inside

Confirm flap overlap, magnet placement and insert height on the loaded sample. A satisfying closure should not require pressure against a bottle cap or another fragile component.

Customization & approval

Build a specification that can be approved

📐

Internal size and clearance

State usable internal length × width × height, product weight and the arrangement of every component. Include the insert and the space needed to lift the product out.

✨

Paper, print and finishing

Compare wrapping paper, printed color, foil, embossing and spot UV on physical references where possible. Fine details, deep colors and textured papers need artwork review.

🧩

Insert and closure approval

Choose paperboard, foam or fabric-covered support according to the item. Approve the fitted pack, opening force, visible seams and surface protection before bulk production.
