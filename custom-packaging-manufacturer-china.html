Frequently Asked

Why source from a China manufacturer?

A China-based factory like iColorPacks offers direct-factory pricing without agent markups, access to specialized finishing like foil stamping and embossing, and flexible low MOQ production that many local suppliers cannot match.

How do you handle shipping to the US or Europe?

We offer Global DDP shipping by sea, air, or express courier, meaning duties and delivery are handled door-to-door, including direct shipment to Amazon FBA warehouses.

How do I know the quality will be consistent?

We confirm the material, structure, finish and inspection requirements before production, then compare finished packaging with the approved specification and sample.

What if I don't have a finished design yet?

Our in-house design team provides free structural design support and a 3D mockup within 24 hours, so you can start from a concept or reference image.

Samples, ordering & delivery

Reply within 1 hour · Quotation within 24 hours. Share the size, quantity, artwork and delivery destination for your quotation.

Free reference samples

Paper swatches and existing samples from our sample room are free for reviewing styles and quality. You only pay the shipping cost. Existing samples do not include new custom branding.

Custom samples

Custom samples require a sample fee and shipping charge. The sample fee is refundable after you place the bulk production order. Sample shipping is paid separately and is not included in the sample-fee refund. The sample specification, fee and refund arrangement are recorded in your quotation.

Order & payment

Confirm the specification and quotation, review the sample, approve the artwork and then proceed to bulk production. Payment method, currency, deposit and balance schedule are confirmed in the written order before payment. Use only the payment details confirmed with Jennifer.

Production & shipping

Sample-making, bulk production and transit are scheduled separately. The production start date follows the agreed approval and payment conditions. Sea, train, air and express options can be reviewed for your destination. FOB, CIF, DAP or DDP availability and included charges are confirmed in the quotation. Send your country, postcode and required arrival date.
