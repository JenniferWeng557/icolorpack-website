Questions before you order

Grosgrain ribbon paper bags: buying questions

How do I measure a paper bag?

Give the front width, side gusset and body height. State handle length separately and include the dimensions of the item or retail box that must pass through the opening.

Does thicker paper always carry more?

No. Handle anchoring, reinforcement, base construction and the shape of the load also matter. Confirm performance using a finished sample and the intended contents.

Can I get help with artwork and a quotation?

Yes. Free design support and quotations within 24 hours are available. Share your quantity, bag size, filled weight, branding references and delivery destination.
