Coordinate the look, the load and the specification

Size and carrying method

Specify width × gusset × height, product dimensions and the expected filled weight. State whether handle length should suit hand carrying or over-arm use.

🎨

Print and handle matching

Review paper, lamination, ribbon or other handles together. Printed colors and metallic effects should be evaluated on the selected paper, not only in a digital mockup.

Finished-bag approval

Check the top reinforcement, handle attachment and base with representative contents. Review crease lines, surface marking and packing method before approving the sample.

Questions before you order

Luxury paper bags: buying questions

How do I measure a paper bag?

Give the front width, side gusset and body height. State handle length separately and include the dimensions of the item or retail box that must pass through the opening.

Does thicker paper always carry more?

No. Handle anchoring, reinforcement, base construction and the shape of the load also matter. Confirm performance using a finished sample and the intended contents.

Can I get help with artwork and a quotation?

Yes. Free design support and quotations within 24 hours are available. Share your quantity, bag size, filled weight, branding references and delivery destination.
