Questions before you order

Luxury paper bags: buying questions

How do I measure a paper bag?

Give the front width, side gusset and body height. State handle length separately and include the dimensions of the item or retail box that must pass through the opening.

Does thicker paper always carry more?

No. Handle anchoring, reinforcement, base construction and the shape of the load also matter. Confirm performance using a finished sample and the intended contents.

Should I choose 250gsm or 300gsm paper?

Compare finished samples with the same dimensions, handles and reinforcement. Paper GSM alone does not establish carrying capacity. Tell us the filled weight and product size so the construction can be reviewed together.

Can ribbon handles pass through punched holes without metal eyelets?

Specify punched-hole ribbon handles and no metal eyelets in your brief. Confirm the hole position, ribbon width, handle drop and reinforcement on the drawing and finished sample.

Why is a 100-piece order more expensive per bag?

Setup and tooling costs are spread across fewer pieces. Request 100 / 500 / 1,000-piece comparisons using the same specification, with setup, sample and freight charges listed separately.

Can I get help with artwork and a quotation?

Yes. Send your requirements for artwork review and a specification-based quotation. Share your quantity, bag size, filled weight, branding references and delivery destination.
