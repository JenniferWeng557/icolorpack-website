Buyer Questions

Before sampling

What is the difference between matte and anti-scuff film?

Anti-scuff film is formulated to reduce visible handling marks on dark or saturated colors.

Can these bags carry shoe boxes?

Yes. We calculate the paper, base card and handle construction for the box dimensions and weight.

Can the inside be printed?

Yes. Interior flood color or custom graphics can be added before bag conversion.

Review the finish on your artwork

Dark solids, pale colors and fine type can look different under matte lamination. Supply reference colors and identify the surfaces most likely to be handled.

✓

Check folds and rub marks

Inspect the gussets, top fold and base after assembly. Review scuff appearance and crease behavior on the selected paper; a matte finish is not automatically scratch-proof.

Questions before you order

Matte laminated paper bags: buying questions

How do I measure a paper bag?

Give the front width, side gusset and body height. State handle length separately and include the dimensions of the item or retail box that must pass through the opening.

Does thicker paper always carry more?

No. Handle anchoring, reinforcement, base construction and the shape of the load also matter. Confirm performance using a finished sample and the intended contents.

Can I get help with artwork and a quotation?

Yes. Free design support and quotations within 24 hours are available. Share your quantity, bag size, filled weight, branding references and delivery destination.
