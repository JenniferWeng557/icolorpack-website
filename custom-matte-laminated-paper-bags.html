Buyer Questions

Before sampling

What is the difference between matte and anti-scuff film?

Anti-scuff film is formulated to reduce visible handling marks on dark or saturated colors.

Can these bags carry shoe boxes?

Yes. We calculate the paper, base card and handle construction for the box dimensions and weight.

Can the inside be printed?

Yes. Interior flood color or custom graphics can be added before bag conversion.
