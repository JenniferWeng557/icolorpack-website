Specification guide

Quote from finished bag dimensions

Provide width, height and gusset in the order requested, plus expected product load. If the bag carries a rigid box, share the box's finished dimensions and packed weight.

Recycled content, certifications, testing and market requirements must be stated; they are not assumed from the appearance of a reference image.

Buyer questions

Ribbon handle bag FAQ

What size should I order?

Share the dimensions of the box or products being carried and the preferred clearance. We can review the bag width, height and gusset around that load.

How is handle strength confirmed?

Paper, reinforcement, attachment and packed weight are reviewed together. When a documented load requirement matters, specify it before sampling.

Can the bag match our gift boxes?

Yes. A shared color and finish direction can be developed, while allowing for the different materials and production tolerances of bags and boxes.
