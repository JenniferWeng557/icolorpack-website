Kraft Bag Cost Factors

Strategic Pricing Insights for Wholesale

Machine vs. Hand Made

High-volume machine-made bags are extremely cost-effective. Hand-made kraft bags allow for luxury finishes (foil, spot UV) but carry a higher labor cost.

Quantity Scale

As a basic retail item,kraft bags rely on economy of scale. Unit prices drop by up to 60% when moving from 500 units to 5,000+ units.

Printing Complexity

Flexo printing is ideal for high-volume 1-2 color designs. For multi-color, full-bleed artwork, offset printing provides better precision at a slightly higher cost.

Handle Material

Twisted paper handles are the industry standard for durability and eco-compliance. Upgrading to cotton or ribbon adds a luxury boutique touch.
