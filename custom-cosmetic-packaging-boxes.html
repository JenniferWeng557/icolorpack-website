Buyer questions

Cosmetic box FAQ

Can one insert hold jars, bottles and tubes together?

Yes. Send dimensions and filled weights for each component. We also need the preferred presentation direction and how the customer should remove each item.

Can you match our campaign colors?

We can review artwork and reference samples. Exact appearance varies by paper and process, so the proofing route and acceptable tolerance are agreed before production.

Can I test the fit before ordering in bulk?

Yes. A structural sample is useful for fit and usability; a printed sample may be added when surface appearance must also be approved.
